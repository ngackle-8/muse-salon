ABOUT MUSE

A space for stylists.
A place to feel like yourself.

Muse brings independent stylists together under one roof, giving each artist the freedom to create their own experience while being part of a shared space.

We are an all inclusive and luxury salon that provides top of the line service including skin care, hair, massage, and makeup catering to all your self-care needs.
