MEET GABRIELLA

Gabriella is the owner of Muse and a master hairstylist specializing in customized color, dimensional blonding, and corrective color. Her approach blends modern, wearable hair with a relaxed and elevated client experience.

ABOUT

Elevated hair.
Effortlessly you.

Hi, I’m Gabriella, owner and stylist at Muse Salon & Spa.

I’m passionate about creating beautiful, effortless hair that feels elevated while still feeling like you.

With a focus on customized color, dimensional blonding, corrective color, and healthy hair, I love creating looks that are modern, wearable, and tailored to each client.

More than anything, I want every visit to Muse to feel relaxed, welcoming, and a little luxurious.

I’m so grateful to do what I love and to have created a space where my clients can feel comfortable, confident, and taken care of.
