MEET LEXI

Lexi specializes in lived-in color and blonding, creating effortless, dimensional looks that feel natural, polished, and uniquely you. With years of experience in both hair and makeup, she brings an eye for detail and a warm, personal approach to every appointment.

ABOUT

Effortless color.
Personal touch.

With 8 years behind the chair as a hairstylist and 10 years as a makeup artist, beauty has always been a passion of mine.

I specialize in lived-in color and blonding, creating effortless, dimensional looks that feel beautiful, natural, and uniquely you.

Outside of the salon, I’m a proud girl mom-to-be, welcoming my little girl in 2027. When I’m not creating beautiful hair, you can usually find me kayaking, exploring the sand dunes, taking weekend trips, or spending time with my fiancé and our dog.

I’m also a coffee lover and always up for trying a new coffee shop or finding a new favorite place to eat. I love the little adventures, good food, quality time with the people I love, and making memories along the way.

I can’t wait to meet you, get to know you, and help you feel your absolute best in my chair.
