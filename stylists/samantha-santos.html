ABOUT

Behind
the chair.

Hi, I’m Sammie Santos, a licensed cosmetologist based in Richland, Washington, with 7 years of experience behind the chair. I specialize in lived-in color, rich reds, warm coppers, dimensional brunettes, and corrective color.

I love creating looks that feel natural, flattering, and uniquely tailored to each client. For me, doing hair is about more than the finished result. I want every person in my chair to feel comfortable, cared for, and confident.

My faith in Jesus is an important part of who I am and shapes the way I approach my work and the relationships I build with my clients.

Outside of hair, I’m an avid reader and keep a community library where clients can borrow, return, and donate books. It’s one of my favorite ways to make my space feel welcoming, personal, and connected.

Whether you’re coming in for a major color correction, a lived-in refresh, a bold copper or brunette transformation, or simply some time to yourself, my goal is for you to leave feeling like the best version of you.
