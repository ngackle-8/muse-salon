SALON POLICIES

A few things
to know.

We want every visit to Muse to feel comfortable, relaxed, and seamless. Please review our salon policies before your appointment.

01

Child-Free Environment

For safety and insurance purposes, Muse Salon & Spa is a child-free salon. We appreciate your understanding as we work to maintain a relaxing and safe environment for everyone.

02

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made within 48 hours of your scheduled appointment are subject to a cancellation fee. Please contact your provider personally with any questions regarding cancellations or rescheduling.

03

Appointment Guidelines

Please arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment. Late arrivals may result in a shortened service or the need to reschedule.

Consultations are recommended for major color changes or services that are new to you.

04

Health & Safety

We maintain high standards of cleanliness and sanitation throughout the salon. Please inform your provider of any allergies or skin sensitivities prior to your service.

QUESTIONS?

Talk to your
stylist.

Questions about cancellations, rescheduling, or your appointment? Please contact your individual provider directly.
